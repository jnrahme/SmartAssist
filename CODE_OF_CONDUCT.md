# SmartAssist Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SmartAssist should be useful, direct, and respectful.

We want contributors, users, and maintainers to feel safe asking questions,
sharing feedback, and proposing changes. That means we expect everyone in this
repo to act with professionalism and basic kindness.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and reviews
- Critique ideas and code, not people
- Share context generously, especially with first-time contributors
- Assume good intent until behavior proves otherwise
- Help keep discussions actionable and on-topic

## Unacceptable Behavior

These behaviors are not welcome here.

- Harassment, intimidation, or threats
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent
- Sexual language or unwanted sexual attention
- Repeated bad-faith arguments that derail the project
- Discrimination based on identity, background, or protected status

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in public project spaces, including issues,
pull requests, review comments, and any project-linked discussion channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a serious conduct problem, email:

- joey@rahme.dev

Please include links, screenshots, or any context that will help with review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or ban
participants whose behavior harms the project.

The goal is to protect the project and the people participating in it.

## Project Principle

SmartAssist is built around learning from feedback. The community should work
the same way: direct, calm, and focused on improving the work.
